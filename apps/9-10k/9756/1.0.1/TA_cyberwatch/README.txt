Cyberwatch Add-on

Collects assets, vulnerabilities, compliance findings and security issues from
the Cyberwatch API and normalizes vulnerabilities to the Splunk Common
Information Model.

Full documentation, including the CIM mapping table and troubleshooting, is in
README.md in this directory, and on the Splunkbase page of the add-on.
