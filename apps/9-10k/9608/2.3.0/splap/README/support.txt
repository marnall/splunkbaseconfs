SPLap support runbook

Symptom: app opens to a setup page
  Cause: another app or a local app.conf override added a setup view. SPLap ships none.
  Fix: remove local/app.conf overrides under etc/apps/splap/local; Install Doctor > "No blocking setup redirect".

Symptom: Pit Wall shows "Asset failed"
  Cause: appserver/static/demo assets missing or modified.
  Fix: Install Doctor > "Demo manifest and asset checksums"; re-install the SPLap package.

Symptom: panels show "Permission denied"
  Cause: role cannot read app knowledge objects.
  Fix: metadata/default.meta grants read to *. Check local.meta overrides and the role's app access.

Symptom: panels show "Search failed"
  Fix: click SHOW SPL > Open in Search and read the job messages. Confirm lookups exist under
  etc/apps/splap/lookups and transforms.conf definitions are readable (Install Doctor).

Symptom: a small "Did SPLap earn a rating?" card keeps appearing
  Cause: browser localStorage is cleared between visits, so the answer cannot be remembered.
  Fix: press "Not now" (snoozes 30 days, stops after two), or allow site data for the Splunk host.

Symptom: replay does not move
  Cause: prefers-reduced-motion is enabled (replay starts paused by design) or the browser tab is throttled.
  Fix: press Play or use the scrubber; keyboard Space toggles play.

Symptom: Install Doctor "Rows scanned/Runtime" over target
  Cause: search head under load. Demo searches read four small lookups and should finish well under 2.5 s.

Collecting diagnostics
  Install Doctor > COPY (secret-redacted JSON). Email to lc@federal.ai with Splunk version, browser, and the failing screen.

Uninstall
  $SPLUNK_HOME/bin/splunk remove app splap ; SPLap creates no indexes, inputs, tokens or roles.
